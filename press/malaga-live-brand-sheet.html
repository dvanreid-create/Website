Málaga Live — the pulse of live music, Málaga city & the Costa del Sol. Primary lockup: play badge + wordmark.

Primary lockup PNG (Anton wordmark + Inter tagline), exact brand colours: MÁLAGA #0B5E8A · LIVE #E8662A · tagline #143A4E.

Lockups

Horizontal lockup for headers, newsletter masthead, and channel banners. On dark, "Málaga" goes white and the badge flips to a white tile. The square badge alone is the profile picture and the video watermark.

Sea blue badge + coral play triangle are the core. Navy is for text and the one-colour logo; amber is an accent for highlights and "free" tags — use sparingly.

Typography

WordmarkAnton (Google Fonts, free for commercial use) · all caps · letter-spacing ~1px. fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton
HeadlinesAnton, or Archivo Bold for longer headers
Body / captionsInter (or Archivo) Regular & Medium
Accent on áAlways keep the accent: Málaga, never "Malaga"

Clear space & usage

Do
  • Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the badge.
  • Use the badge alone wherever space is tight (avatars, watermarks, favicons).
  • Use the mono version for one-colour print or on busy photos.
Don't
  • Recolour the play triangle to anything but coral (or coral-on-white).
  • Stretch, skew, add shadows, outlines, or gradients.
  • Set the wordmark in a different font or drop the á accent.
